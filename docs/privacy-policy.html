Prinsip Utama Privasi Kami
Mankeu dirancang dengan filosofi Offline-First & Local-First. Seluruh data keuangan tani, buku kas, kalkulasi BEP/HPP, dan laporan Anda tersimpan secara lokal di perangkat Anda sendiri. Aplikasi ini tidak memuat iklan, tidak menggunakan pelacak (trackers), dan tidak mengumpulkan telemetri pihak ketiga.

1. Ringkasan & Filosofi Privasi

Mankeu menghargai dan melindungi privasi pelaku usaha mikro, kecil, menengah (UMKM), serta seluruh pengguna aplikasi Mankeu. Dokumen kebijakan privasi ini menjelaskan bagaimana data Anda dikelola secara transparan dan aman saat menggunakan aplikasi kami.

2. Pengolahan Data & Penyimpanan Lokal (Offline-First)

Semua data yang Anda masukkan ke dalam aplikasi — termasuk namun tidak terbatas pada:

  • Daftar Usaha, Cabang, dan Unit Usaha
  • Buku Catatan Kas (Realisasi Transaksi & Proyeksi Anggaran/RAB)
  • Data Penjualan, Biaya Produksi, dan Kalkulasi Finansial (HPP, BEP, Margin Laba)
  • Pengaturan Preferensi Pengguna

disimpan secara lokal di dalam basis data SQLite (Room) internal perangkat Anda. Data finansial tersebut TIDAK PERNAH dikirimkan, disimpan, atau dianalisis di server milik pengembang.

3. Autentikasi Google Sign-In & Cadangan Awan (Google Drive)

Aplikasi menyediakan fitur cadangan awan terenkripsi (Cloud Backup) yang bersifat opsional:

  • Masuk dengan Google (Google Sign-In): Digunakan semata-mata untuk mengautentikasi akun Google Anda jika Anda memilih untuk mencadangkan data ke Google Drive Anda sendiri. Data identitas (Nama dan Email) hanya digunakan untuk menampilkan status akun di layar Pengaturan Akun.
  • Enkripsi Sisi Klien (Client-Side AES-256-GCM): Berkas cadangan (.aabk) dienkripsi langsung di perangkat Anda menggunakan algoritma standar militer AES-256-GCM dengan kata sandi (passphrase) yang Anda tentukan sendiri sebelum diunggah.
  • Folder Terisolasi (drive.appdata): Berkas cadangan terenkripsi disimpan di folder khusus aplikasi yang terisolasi di Google Drive pengguna. Pihak pengembang maupun pihak ketiga tidak memiliki akses ke berkas maupun kunci dekripsi Anda.

4. Pemrosesan Berkas Spreadsheet Excel (.xlsx)

Aplikasi mendukung impor dan ekspor data buku kas melalui berkas Excel (.xlsx):

  • Seluruh proses pembacaan, validasi, dan pencocokan semantik (semantic classification) dilakukan 100% secara lokal di perangkat Anda (on-device processing) tanpa mengunggah berkas ke internet.
  • Aplikasi hanya membaca berkas yang Anda pilih secara sadar melalui antarmuka pemilih berkas sistem (Storage Access Framework).

5. Nol Iklan & Nol Pelacak Pihak Ketiga (Zero Telemetry)

Per komitmen privasi kami:

  • Aplikasi 100% bebas dari segala bentuk iklan (No Ads).
  • Aplikasi TIDAK memuat SDK pelacak analitik (seperti Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, atau penyedia telemetri perilaku pengguna lainnya).
  • Data penggunaan aplikasi Anda tidak dimonetisasi atau dibagikan ke pihak ketiga mana pun.

6. Izin Akses Perangkat (Device Permissions)

7. Kendali, Retensi, dan Penghapusan Data

Anda memiliki kedaulatan penuh atas seluruh data Anda:

  • Hapus satu catatan: ketuk Hapus pada kartu catatan.
  • Hapus semua data: Pengaturan > Hapus semua data. Semua usaha, unit, catatan, rencana usaha, stok, riwayat impor, salinan pemulihan otomatis, dan nama lokal di HP dihapus. Bila Anda masuk dengan Google, opsi yang sama juga menghapus semua cadangan dan berkas sinkronisasi Mankeu di folder aplikasi Google Drive Anda, menghapus akun masuk Mankeu (Firebase Authentication), dan mencabut izin Google Drive. Berkas lain di Google Drive Anda tidak tersentuh.
  • Tanpa membuka aplikasi: hapus data aplikasi lewat Setelan Android > Aplikasi > Mankeu > Penyimpanan, atau copot aplikasi. Cadangan di Google Drive dapat dihapus lewat Google Drive di web: Setelan > Kelola aplikasi > Mankeu > Hapus data aplikasi tersembunyi.
  • Permintaan lewat email: bila tidak bisa memakai cara di atas, kirim email ke alamat kontak di bawah dengan alamat Google yang Anda pakai untuk masuk. Kami hanya dapat menghapus akun masuk (Firebase Authentication); data keuangan Anda tidak pernah sampai ke server kami.
  • Pencabutan Akses Google: Anda dapat keluar akun (Sign Out) kapan saja atau mencabut izin akses Mankeu melalui laman Akun Google Keamanan Anda.

Our Core Privacy Invariant
Mankeu is engineered with an Offline-First & Local-First architecture. All your farm financial records, cash flow entries, BEP/HPP calculations, and reports reside strictly on your local device. This application contains zero ads, zero tracking SDKs, and zero third-party telemetry.

1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

Mankeu respects and protects the privacy of small business owners, MSME entrepreneurs, and all users of Mankeu. This policy details how your data is handled transparently and securely.

2. Data Processing & Local Storage (Offline-First)

All data entered into the application — including but not limited to:

  • Business Entities, Outlets, Branches, and Projects
  • Cash Flow Records (Actual Realized Transactions & Budget Projections)
  • Sales Figures, Production Costs, and Financial Metrics (HPP, BEP, Profit Margin)
  • User Preferences & Application Settings

is stored locally in the on-device SQLite (Room) database. Your financial records are NEVER transmitted to, stored on, or analyzed by developer-owned servers.

3. Google Sign-In & Encrypted Cloud Backup (Google Drive)

The application provides an optional encrypted cloud backup feature:

  • Google Sign-In: Used solely to authenticate your Google account if you choose to back up data to your personal Google Drive. Profile metadata (Name and Email) is used exclusively to display account status on the Settings screen.
  • Client-Side AES-256-GCM Encryption: Backup archives (.aabk) are encrypted on your device using industry-standard AES-256-GCM with a user-supplied secret passphrase prior to transmission.
  • Isolated App Storage (drive.appdata): Encrypted archives are stored in an isolated, private application folder on your Google Drive. Neither the developer nor third parties have access to your backup files or decryption keys.

4. Excel Spreadsheet Processing (.xlsx)

The application supports importing and exporting farm financial workbooks in Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) format:

  • All spreadsheet parsing, verification, and semantic matching are executed 100% locally on your device (on-device processing) without internet transmission.
  • The application only accesses files explicitly chosen by the user via the Android Storage Access Framework.

5. Zero Advertisements & Zero Third-Party Telemetry

Under our strict privacy guidelines:

  • The application is 100% ad-free.
  • The application DOES NOT include any analytics or tracking SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, or behavioral trackers).
  • No user behavior data is ever collected, monetized, or shared with data brokers.

6. Device Permissions

Permission Purpose & Usage
INTERNET Optional — utilized strictly when the user initiates encrypted backup or restore operations with Google Drive APIs.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks network connectivity before attempting Google Drive synchronization.
Storage Access Framework (SAF) Enables user-selected Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx) and encrypted backup (.aabk) file picking and saving.

7. Data Sovereignty, Retention, and Deletion

You maintain complete ownership of your data at all times:

  • Delete one record: tap Delete on the record card.
  • Delete all data: Settings > Delete all data. Every business, unit, record, business plan, stock item, import history, automatic recovery copy and local name on the phone is deleted. If you signed in with Google, the same option also deletes every Mankeu backup and sync file in your Google Drive app folder, deletes the Mankeu sign-in account (Firebase Authentication), and revokes Google Drive access. Other files in your Google Drive are not touched.
  • Without opening the app: clear the app's data in Android Settings > Apps > Mankeu > Storage, or uninstall it. Google Drive backups can be removed on Google Drive web: Settings > Manage apps > Mankeu > Delete hidden app data.
  • Request by email: if none of the above is possible, email the contact address below from the Google address you signed in with. We can only delete the sign-in account (Firebase Authentication); your financial data never reaches our servers.
  • Revoking Google Access: You can sign out at any time or revoke application permissions directly via your Google Account Security Dashboard.

8. Children's Privacy

Mankeu is a professional business accounting and financial calculator designed for adults (18+). It does not knowingly collect any data from children.
